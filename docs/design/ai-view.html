How the data gets decoded

  • FortisBC bill (PDF) → text extraction on the board, then the billing period, GJ, cost and meter readings. Every parsed field is shown before it is imported; anything unsure is marked needs a look rather than guessed.
  • CSV → columns detected (date, usage, unit), previewed as a table, imported on your confirmation.
  • Manual reading → one number and a timestamp; the difference from the previous reading becomes a measured consumption over that exact interval.
  • Everything lands in one small database on the board (ai-data/gas.db), which is what the nightly fit reads. Original files are kept beside it.

Decisions to confirm before building

  • Name: sidebar “AI”, tiles “Automations” and “AI data”. Alternatives: “Intelligence”, “Models”.
  • Upload limits: PDF and CSV, 10 MB a file, authenticated users only (same login as the dashboard).
  • Unit for readings: the meter counts ft³; bills are in GJ. Both are kept, converted for display.
  • Where the fit runs: nightly, in the forecast venv, beside the electricity forecast.
