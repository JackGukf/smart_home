The biggest chart of the three, and room for the cost line. But one fuel is always hidden, and the Home view is meant to be read without touching it.

My suggestion: A. Home is a glance, and A is the only one where both fuels are visible at once with their shapes comparable. B is the fallback if the bars feel too small on the wall panel — it is the same content, laid out to give the charts width. Either takes the same work; C is less code but hides half the answer.
