The Voice Panel, as apps

The panel opens on a launcher — the time, the day and the house at a glance, over six app tiles — instead of a Home page with a ≡ menu. Each app is the screen that is already built and owner-tested; only how you get to it changes. Tap a tile to open it, the back button or a swipe to the right to return. Names, rooms and sensors are this house's; on/off states and the front-yard alert are examples.

Checked against the board

No RAM

App icons

Drawn once by render-panel-icons.py and stored in flash, like the lamps. Six tiles replace the drawer's five buttons.

In ESPHome's LVGL

Swipe back

on_swipe_right exists. It must not fire inside the brightness bar or while a list scrolls, so it goes on the app background only.

Measure first

Slide transitions

LVGL can animate a screen change, but animation redraws the whole screen for its duration — the load that disturbed this RGB display before. Added last, and only if it stays clean.

Tight margin

Memory

Internal heap idles at ~75 KB and fell to 35 KB in real use; a camera frame costs ~21 KB, a voice answer ~10 KB. The launcher must not add standing widgets beyond the drawer it replaces.

Build order

  1. Launcher and navigationLauncher page with plain tiles, back button on every app, the drawer removed; open/leave hooks moved. Everything inside the apps untouched.
  2. Climate as its own appThe thermostat leaves the Lights app for a larger Climate screen with room temperature and humidity.
  3. App icons and badgesTinted tiles rendered into flash; badges fed by states the panel already receives — lights on, target temperature, security alerts.
  4. Swipe right to go backOn app backgrounds only, checked against the brightness bar and the scrolling light list.
  5. Sleep and wakeDim after idle, wake on touch to the launcher; "Okay Nabu" shows a listening overlay on any screen.

Decisions

Launcher instead of the ≡ menuFor approvalThe panel starts on the launcher; apps return to it. Waveshare's demo was described as app-style — a photo of its launcher would let the tiles match it.
Six appsFor approvalLights, Climate, Scenes, Security, Cameras, Wall panel — Climate split out of Home.
Launcher glanceFor approvalLarge clock, the day, and the inside temperature from the ecobee. No weather: the panel has no forecast today.
Going backFor approvalBack button always; swipe right as well. Slide animations only if the display stays clean.
Movie modeDecidedLiving room switch 2 off, Living room cabinet LED off, Living room ambient light on.
Light cardsDecidedThe six lights on the dashboard's Lights card, all on/off; dimmer brightness on the full-screen light page.
Wall-panel viewsDecidedHome, Cameras, Security, Devices, Climate and Status — only the hallway panel follows them.
CamerasDecidedFront door and garage, near-live through the board's camera relay, opening on the saved last view.
